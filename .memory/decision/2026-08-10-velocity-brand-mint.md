Velocity brand semantic tokens (`background.brand`, `accent.*`, `brand.primary`/`secondary`, `content.brand`, `border.brand`/`focus`, `surface.brand-tint`/`brand-emphasis`) now use mint instead of yellow, matching the Runspot mint scale (light: 500/400/200/700; dark: 400/500/600; tint/emphasis from mint.500).
